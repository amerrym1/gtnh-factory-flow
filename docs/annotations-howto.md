# Notes, boxes and arrows: how to

1. **Add**: right-click empty board, then pick Add sticky note, Add box or Add arrow.
2. **Move**: drag a note anywhere; drag a box by its border; drag an arrow by its line.
3. **Resize a box**: drag a corner, or the edge just outside its border.
4. **Reshape an arrow**: select it or point at it, then drag either square end handle (drag one past the other to flip it).
5. **Restyle a box**: right-click its border, then Colour, Fill (tint, solid, none), Border (solid, dashed) or Marks (dots, grid, graph, ruled, hatch).
6. **Restyle an arrow**: right-click its line, then Colour, Thickness (thin, normal, thick) or Heads (one end, both ends).
7. **Notes**: click to write; right-click for colour, text size and delete.
8. **Delete**: right-click then Delete, or select and press Delete.
9. **Several at once**: Shift-click or drag a marquee; they move and delete together. Ctrl+Z undoes any of it.
10. Pictures are no longer supported, and zones (polygons) are shown but cannot be added.
